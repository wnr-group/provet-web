# Homepage hero video

`provet-hero-1280.mp4` (the video) and `provet-hero-poster.jpg` (its poster) are one
26-second silent loop cut from five Pexels clips, each trimmed to six seconds and crossfaded:

1. White broiler hens, close-up - https://www.pexels.com/video/poultry-farming-of-chickens-as-business-3370949/
2. Holstein dairy herd on pasture - https://www.pexels.com/video/farmers-oversee-cattle-in-lush-pasture-37321694/
3. Aquaculture (shrimp) ponds with aerators, aerial - https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-shot-of-ponds-5844662/
4. Coastal fish farm, aerial - https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-view-of-coastal-fish-farm-aquaculture-32322608/
5. School of fish, open water - https://www.pexels.com/video/majestic-school-of-fish-swimming-underwater-33179653/
