# Range logos

Provet's own wordmarks for its two product ranges, taken from provet.in:

- `avinova-logo.png` (800 x 149) - https://provet.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/poultry_logo.png
- `blunova-logo.png` (800 x 144) - https://provet.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/aqua_logo.png

Wired to the ranges by slug in `lib/categoryTheme.js` (`logo`) and drawn by
`components/ui/RangeLogo.js`. A replacement keeps the file name, and its new pixel size goes in `logo`.
